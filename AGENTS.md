# Repository instructions

## Portfolio and career reference documents

These rules are mandatory when editing `CONTEXT.md` or `PROJECTS.md`.

- Read the relevant document and its maintenance/editing guidelines before changing it. Follow and retain those guidelines in subsequent rewrites.
- Maintain current-state references, not conversation logs. Update information in place; remove superseded facts, answered questions, repeated advice, and temporary execution details.
- Keep writing concise, factual, and self-contained. Avoid conversational history, cross-project narrative transitions, and repetitive caveats.
- Distinguish user statements, source evidence, assistant proposals, and approved decisions. Do not invent details or promote uncertain claims into established facts.
- Treat [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md) and [PROJECTS.md](PROJECTS.md) as linked references. Before editing either, read both documents and their guidelines; after editing, check the other for affected summaries, ownership, review status, recommendations, and open questions. Update affected sections in the same task, but do not change an unaffected file merely to mark it synchronized.
- Use `PROJECTS.md` as the source of project-specific facts and `CONTEXT.md` for their durable career/portfolio implications. Maintain reciprocal links, avoid duplicating detailed entries, and resolve contradictions in place while preserving source attribution and uncertainty. New evidence does not automatically approve a shortlist or career direction.

### `CONTEXT.md`

- Store durable goals, preferences, constraints, decisions, career context, and unresolved questions.
- Keep detailed project information in `PROJECTS.md`; link to it instead of duplicating entries.

### `PROJECTS.md`

- Maintain an inventory for portfolio content preparation, not finished portfolio copy or a continuous story.
- Keep each project understandable independently. Group related touchpoints under their event where appropriate.
- Separate Kent's contributions from collaborators' work and preserve accurate credits.
- Preserve metric units and reporting periods. Do not conflate plays, submissions, attendance, or unique users.
- Distinguish measurements, simulations, observations, and user testing. Put unresolved details and supporting-material requests in concise **To add** lists.
- Do not rank, shortlist, or remove projects unless explicitly requested.
- Confirm publication permissions before moving client assets or confidential details into public-facing portfolio content.

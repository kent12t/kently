## First handoff: Galilean Mission

Supply an existing folder or a small set of files; no polished presentation is needed. Private material can remain outside this repository, with access provided for review.

1. **Experience:** One short clip showing the room, visitor action, and game response. Note date, hardware, and whether it is a prototype or final installation.
2. **Space:** Two or three spatial renders with matching built-space photos and one layout. Identify Kent's work versus supplied assets.
3. **Visual content:** One moon-animation excerpt, plus a procedural/working view if available.
4. **Iteration:** One testing observation and its corresponding change; include before/after footage or screenshots if they exist. State who tested and how.
5. **Operation:** A redacted SOP/flowchart excerpt and any existing system diagram. Remove credentials, addresses, device identifiers, and sensitive operational details.
6. **Outcome and credits:** Source for the first-five-week participation count, named collaborators, and publication permissions.

Minimum useful first batch: items 1–3. The rest can follow; missing evidence should narrow claims, not trigger invented reconstruction.

## Review method once assets arrive

- Evaluate visual hierarchy, craft, feedback, pacing, legibility, and coherence between screen and space.
- For live interfaces, inspect representative mobile/desktop flows, keyboard access, and reduced-motion behaviour where applicable. Separate observed problems from untested assumptions.
- Compare proposal, iteration, and final execution. Record specific decisions, not just a gallery of outputs.
- Confirm personal contribution, collaborators, evidence type, and permission for each selected item.
- Then revise the first case study and propose a small selection for Kent's approval. Do not rank work based on repository complexity or recency alone.

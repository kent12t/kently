## Short introduction

### I design experiences for screens and spaces.

Interactive prototypes, playful interfaces, and environments brought to life.

I'm Kent, a designer and creative technologist working across interactive interfaces, 3D, and physical spaces. I turn ideas into experiences people can explore, play with, and take part in—with an eye for both how they look and how they work in practice.

## About me

I like making things people can do something with: a playful microsite, an interactive exhibition, a digital tool that feels satisfying to use. Seeing someone engage with something I've helped create is the part that makes the work worthwhile.

My background is in industrial design, with a minor in interactive media at NUS. My work now moves between interface design, interactive prototyping, spatial design, and 3D imagery. Sometimes that means shaping a screen-based journey; sometimes it means working out where the screen belongs, how someone reaches it, and what happens around it.

What connects those disciplines is attention to the experience. I care about the placement of a button, the timing of feedback, and what happens when an interaction doesn't go as planned. My work has included refining a card-making interface, adapting camera-based interactions, and designing exhibition spaces that have to accommodate people—not just look good in a render.

I work with code as a design medium, and use AI-assisted development to prototype and implement ideas. In recent work, my focus has been on experience design, system planning, and directing and refining implementation. I collaborate with visual designers, developers, builders, and integration teams to bring those pieces together.

I'm looking for a team where I can keep making thoughtful, playful experiences while growing alongside people I can learn from. I'm especially interested in work that connects digital interaction with the physical world, without being limited to it.

## What I bring

- **An eye for interaction and detail.** I notice the small decisions that make an interface clearer, more responsive, or more enjoyable, and like working through them.
- **A connection between screens and spaces.** I can move between user journeys, interactive prototypes, spatial layouts, and 3D renders while keeping the overall experience in view.
- **Ideas that can become tangible.** I contribute to concepts and wireframes, then help turn them into working experiences rather than stopping at a presentation.
- **Attention beyond the demo.** My work includes input fallbacks, kiosk setup, troubleshooting guidance, and on-site adjustments. All less visible details that support delivery.
